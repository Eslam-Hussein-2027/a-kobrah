# Surface brief: company website (home and every page)

Scope: the whole public site in `front/front-kobrah` (home, about, personal, business, forms, contact, 404). Visitor mode: Persuade on the home page; the inner pages keep the same world with more restraint.

Audience and job: individuals and companies in Libya, equal priority. Action: request a quote for a specific cover (contact form pre-filled), call, or download a form for the branch. Proof on hand: 15+ years, 30+ branches and agents, 25,000+ clients, 14 products, 8 sectors. No photography; nothing fabricated.

Brand pins: navy #1A1C95, blue #0072CE, sky #00A8E8, cyan #22C3F3, the logo gradient; modern, no template look.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The company is a network you can ride: two lines, one for individuals and one for businesses, whose stations are the products, meeting at a services interchange. Refuses the insurer default of stock-photo hero, shield icons and a row of equal product cards.

OWN-WORLD: A printed schematic transit map. White map sheet, thick round-capped lines (sky = individuals, navy = businesses, blue = services trunk, logo gradient only on the interchange and the terminus), white station rings with ink outlines, line bullets (filled circles with the line number), geometric Kufi lettering for station names and headings, a readable Arabic sans for text, tabular figures, hairline rules. Bands of line colour own whole regions (route headers, terminus, footer).

STORY: The visitor sees both lines at once, finds their own line, taps a station to read that cover, and requests a quote for it in one step; on every page the header line marks where they are; every route ends at a terminus that leads to a person (quote, call, branch).
